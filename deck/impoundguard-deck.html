score = 100 × urgency × ( w·scarcity + (1−w)·revenue )
Urgency

Days to the worst document lapsing. Expired pins it to the top.

Revenue

Daily income, relative to the fleet’s highest earner.

Scarcity

Compliant, capable spares for this route, counted from the fleet — zero scores 1, three or more scores 0.

No language model in this ranking — arithmetic, runs offline, cannot fail live. And w is a slider the owner controls: scarcity is counted from the fleet, never assigned by us.
